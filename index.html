ClipMachine

Long videos in.
Short clips out.

ClipMachine is a personal desktop app. It turns a long video into short vertical clips with captions, then publishes the clips the owner picks to their own TikTok account, spaced a few minutes apart, through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

  1. Paste a video linkThe app downloads the video and transcribes what is said.
  2. Get the best moments as clipsVertical 9:16 framing on the speaker, word-by-word captions, a title and a caption with hashtags.
  3. Connect TikTok onceSign in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorize ClipMachine to post to your account.
  4. Publish or schedulePost a clip now, or let the selected clips go out every 3 minutes.
