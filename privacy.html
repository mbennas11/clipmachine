ClipMachine

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

ClipMachine is a personal desktop application that turns long videos into short vertical clips and publishes them to the owner's own TikTok account on a schedule. It runs entirely on the owner's computer. There is no ClipMachine server and no ClipMachine user account.

1. Information we access from TikTok

When you connect your TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, ClipMachine requests these permissions:

  • user.info.basic: your display name, shown in the app so you can see which account is connected.
  • video.publish: permission to publish the clips you schedule to your own TikTok account.

TikTok returns an access token and a refresh token. ClipMachine also reads TikTok's creator information (available privacy levels and comment settings) just before each post, so it publishes with settings your account allows.

2. How the information is used

This information is used only to publish the videos and captions you scheduled, at the times you chose, and to show the result of each post in the app. It is not used for advertising, profiling, analytics or any other purpose.

3. Where the information is stored

Tokens, your display name, your clips, captions and posting schedule are stored only in local files on your own computer. Nothing is sent to ClipMachine's developer or to any third party. Video files are sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official Content Posting API, when a scheduled post is published.

4. Sharing

ClipMachine does not sell, rent or share your information. The only data transfer is the upload of your scheduled videos and captions to TikTok.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stays on your computer until you delete it. You can disconnect ClipMachine at any time by deleting the local file tiktok_token.json or by removing ClipMachine from the authorized apps in your TikTok account settings. Deleting the ClipMachine folder removes all stored data.

6. Security

All communication with TikTok uses HTTPS. The sign-in flow uses OAuth 2.0 with PKCE, and the authorization callback is received only on your own computer (localhost).

7. Children

ClipMachine is not intended for anyone under the minimum age required to hold a TikTok account.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
